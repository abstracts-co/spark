# Spark

A life is about 960 months. Spark shows you how many you have left and helps you use them.

- **Your months.** Say when you were born and see all 960: the ones behind you, this one, and the ones ahead.
- **A scrapbook for each month.** Add photos, video, screenshots and notes to a month and look back on it later. Months with a scrapbook glow on your grid. Your scrapbook is private: only you can open it.
- **Sparks.** A spark is one thing worth doing: an adventure, a bucket-list item or a connection with someone. Plan them for this month or a later one and tick them off.
- **Your AI.** Connect your own AI and it can read your months and plans and suggest sparks. You choose which ones to keep.
- **Share.** Share a month, or your whole Spark, as a post on a/spark when you want to. Take a spark you like from someone else's month.

Start at https://abstracts.co/a/spark
